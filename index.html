Research interests

My research centers on robot learning for manipulators and humanoid robots. I am interested in enabling robots to understand their environment, language, and spatial context, and to perform precise actions from human instructions.

I work with computer vision, machine learning, deep learning, and robotics, including imitation-learning policies, large language models, vision-language models, and vision-language-action models. My long-term goal is to develop and apply foundation models for embodied robotics.

RESEARCH PROJECTOngoing

Imitation-learning failure recovery with vision-language models

Investigating VLMs and LLMs for failure assessment and corrective guidance in place of manual labeling. The goal is to distill visual observations, robot states, and language-based corrections into policies that can recover from and explain failures with lower computational cost than VLA systems.
